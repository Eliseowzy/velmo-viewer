# Five Days in Kyoto

A slow trip through temples, tea houses and quiet side streets, planned for early **November** when the maples start to turn.

## Before you go

- Get an **ICOCA** card for buses and trains
- Book *Katsura Imperial Villa* at least three months ahead
- Pack comfortable shoes — you will walk ~15 km a day

> The best way to see Kyoto is to get a little lost.

## Daily plan

| Day | Morning | Afternoon |
| --- | ------- | --------- |
| 1 | Fushimi Inari, before 7 am | Tōfuku-ji gardens |
| 2 | Arashiyama bamboo grove | Ōkōchi Sansō |
| 3 | Kinkaku-ji | Ryōan-ji rock garden |
| 4 | Philosopher's Path | Nanzen-ji |
| 5 | Nishiki Market | Tea ceremony in Gion |
